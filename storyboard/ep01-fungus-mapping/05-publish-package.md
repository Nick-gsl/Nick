## 4.2 视频描述

### 第 1 段 · 悬念简介（不剧透结论）

```
It has no eyes. No nerves. No brain. And right now — under your shoes —
something is voting on where to grow next. We just started recording it.
What we found does not behave like a plant.
```

### 第 2 段 · 内容概述（3 个看点）

```
Three things this episode unpacks:
- Why fungi pulse with electricity — each species in its own rhythm,
  with a vocabulary specific enough that one researcher counted the "words."
- How a fungus can "remember" where a piece of wood used to be — even after
  every connection has been physically severed.
- The 2024 experiment where the same fungus, same soil, and nine identical
  wood blocks produced two completely different strategies — based only on
  the geometric shape they were arranged in.
```
